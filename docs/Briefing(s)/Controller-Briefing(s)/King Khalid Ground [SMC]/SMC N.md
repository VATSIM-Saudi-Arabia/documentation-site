# OERK_N_GND [SMC N] Briefing Material | Cross the Land: Westbound 2026

!!! success "Covering"
    This section details all the necessary briefing materials for **OERK_N_GND [SMC N]** during Cross the Land: Westbound 2026

!!! Caution "Bandbox"
    During the event, only SMC N and SMC S are going to be online, spliting the DOAR on 33R/15L.

## Designated Area of Responsibility 
**"King Khalid Ground" (OERK_N_GND)** is in charge of all GMC operations west of RWY 33R/15L. Ground North includes the Passenger terminals, Cargo apron, and GA aprons **(Aprons 1, 2, 3, 4, 5, C and G)**.

## Genetal Notes
- Traffic **departig* on 33R shall exit the aprons via R and S. Expect arrivals to enter through Taxiway P.
- If 15 config is in use, traffic shall exit the aprons via P, R, and S. Expect arrivals to enter through Taxiway T.
- Controllers must ensure that taxiways P, R, and S are not blocked and clear when issuing pushback clearances.
- It is important that SMC N and S are in constant coordination to monitor traffic flow and ensure the movements are conflict-free.

### 33's configuration
- **Departures** from Apron 1 and 2 will be cleared by **SMC N** to taxi until holding short of **S via R** and shall be transferred to "King Khalid Ground South" **approaching S** if clear of conflict. Excercise caution for the traffic coming from **S**.

- **Departures** from Apron 3 and 4 will be cleared by **SMC N** to taxi until holding short of **G via S** and shall be transferred to "King Khalid Ground South" **entering G** if clear of conflict. Excercise caution for the traffic coming from **G via R**.

- **Departures** from Apron 5 will be cleared by **SMC N** to taxi until holding short of **G via S**. In case taxiway S is overloaded, **SMC N** shall coordinate with **SMC S** to allow traffic to exit Apron 5 via **taxiway T** before being transferred to "King Khalid Ground South" entering T.

### 15's configuration
- **Departures** from Apron 1 and 2 will be cleared by **SMC N** to taxi until holding short of **G via P** and shall be transferred to "King Khalid Tower East" **approaching G** if clear of conflict. Excercise caution for the traffic coming through **G**.

- **Departures** from Apron 3 and 4 will be cleared by **SMC N** to taxi until holding short of **P via R and G** and shall be transferred to "King Khalid Tower East" **approaching P** if clear of conflict. Excercise caution for the traffic coming through **G**.

- **Departures** from Apron 5 will be cleared by **SMC N** to taxi until holding short of **P via S and G** and shall be transferred to "King Khalid Tower East" **approaching P** if clear of conflict. Excercise caution for the traffic coming through **S and R**.
