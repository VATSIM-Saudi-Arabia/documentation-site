# OERK_S_GND [SMC S] Briefing Material | Cross the Land: Westbound 2026

!!! success "Covering"
    This section details all the necessary briefing materials for **OERK_S_GND [SMC S]** during Cross the Land: Westbound 2026

!!! Caution "Bandbox"
    During the event, only SMC N and SMC S are going to be online, spliting the DOAR on 33R/15L.

## Designated Area of Responsibility 
**"King Khalid Ground" (OERK_S_GND)** is in charge of all GMC operations west of RWY 33R/15L. Ground North includes the International terminals and Royal aprons **(Aprons 6, 7, 8 and 9)**.

## Genetal Notes
- Traffic **departig* on 33R shall exit the aprons via U and V. Expect arrivals to enter through Taxiway T.
- If 15 config is in use, traffic shall exit the aprons via T, U, and V. Expect arrivals to enter through Taxiway T.
- Controllers must ensure that taxiways T, U, and V are not blocked and clear when issuing pushback clearances.
- It is important that SMC S and N are in constant coordination to monitor traffic flow and ensure the movements are conflict-free.

### 33's configuration
- **Departures** from Apron 6B, 6D and 6E will be cleared by **SMC S** to taxi until holding short of **G via V** and shall be transferred to "King Khalid Tower East" **approaching G** if clear of conflict.

- **Departures** from Apron 6A and 6C will be cleared by **SMC S** to taxi until holding short of **V via U and G** and shall be transferred to "King Khalid Tower East" **entering G** if clear of conflict. Excercise caution for the traffic coming from **G**.

- **Departures** from Apron 1,2,3,4,5 will be cleared by **SMC N** to taxi until holding short of **G or T**. Expect traffic to call you holding short of **G** and **T** when coordinating and shall be taxied by **SMC S** via **G to holding short of V**. if clear of conflict, traffic contact "King Khalid Tower East".

### 15's configuration
- **Departures** from Apron 6B, 6D and 6E will be cleared by **SMC S** to taxi until holding short of **S via U and G** and shall be transferred to "King Khalid Ground North" **passing T** if clear of conflict. Excercise caution for the traffic coming through **G via T**.

- **Departures** from Apron 6A and 6C will be cleared by **SMC S** to taxi until holding short of **S via T and G** and shall be transferred to "King Khalid Ground North" **entering G** if clear of conflict. Excercise caution for the traffic coming through **G via U**.

- In case taxiway U is overloaded, **SMC S** shall offload the traffic to exit Apron 6B, 6D and 6E via **taxiway V** and join G before being transferred to "King Khalid Ground North" passing T.
