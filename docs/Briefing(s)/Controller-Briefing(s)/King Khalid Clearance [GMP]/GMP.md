## Designated Area of Responsibility 
"*King Khalid Clearance*" (OERK_DEL) will be responsible of issuing IFR clearances for all departure traffic from Riyadh and the transfer of traffic requesting push to the correct ground controller.

---

## General Notes
- Expect the IFR flight plans to be validated and corrected by "*King Khalid Planner*" (OERK_P_DEL) preemptively.
- IFR clearances in Riyadh include the **departure runway**, the **SID**, the **initial climb**, and **the squawk** using the prescribed phraseology in the **SOP**.
- The initial climb for all SIDs is **8000ft**.
- Departure traffic shall only be cleared on RWY 33R/15L regardless of their stand or requests as SARO will be in use during the whole event to maximize the departure runway capacity.
- **Departure** traffic shall be handed off to "*King Khalid Ground*" (OERK_N_GND) if at **Apron 1, 2, 3, 4, 5** or to "*King Khalid Ground*" (OERK_S_GND) controller if at Apron 6, 7, 8, 9.
