## Designated Area of Responsibility 
"*King Khalid Planner*" (OERK_DEL) will be responsible of validating IFR flight plans for all departure traffic from Riyadh and manage a controlled sequence of traffic efficiently.

---

## General Notes
- IFR flight plans must be **validated and corrected** according the published event routes prior issuing clearance by "*King Khalid Clearance*" (OERK_DEL).
- Ensure traffic are ready for pushback and be transferred to the correct ground controller within **(+5 / -5)** window.
- The pilot has called ready before his TSAT window, set the TOBT to "**now**" by left clicking and selecting "TOBT now".
