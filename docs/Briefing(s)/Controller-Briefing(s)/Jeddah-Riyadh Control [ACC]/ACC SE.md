# OEJD_SE_CTR [ACC SE] Briefing Material | Cross the Land: Westbound 2026

!!! success "Covering"

## Designated Area of Responsibility 

"*Jeddah Control*" (OEJD_SE_CTR) is in charge of the Southeastern Jeddah Sector from SFC-UNL.

---

## Notes

* The traffic flows from the east will be handed off to ACC SE by UAE Radar.
* Controllers are required to use the VATCAN plugin to distinguish event traffic from non-event traffic.
* RVSM procedures and the semi-circular cruising level rule are applicable throughout the OEJD FIR.
   * Standard separation minima within the airspace are:
      * 1,000 ft vertical separation up to FL410 or 10 NM lateral separation.
      * 2,000 ft vertical separation above FL410 or 10 NM lateral separation.
* Any non-event aircraft leaving the Jeddah FIR with event destinations must obtain a release from the appropriate Jeddah Control sector before leaving.   Adjacent ACC sectors are responsible for coordinating the release prior to transfer.
* Issuing arrival clearances for traffic inbound to Riyadh is not the responsibility of Jeddah Control; this duty belongs to Jeddah Control 3.
* A minimum of 15 NM longitudinal spacing must be maintained between enroute aircraft before transfer to the Riyadh Control sector.
* Controllers should regularly review ECFMP updates to remain informed of the latest traffic flow restrictions and measures.
